Appearance

A theme sets colours, corner radius and fonts, and can add its own styles, templates and images. Themes live in themes/<name>/ inside the data directory; a change to a theme's files is live within a second, without a restart. A theme that cannot be loaded is skipped and the standard look is used instead.
